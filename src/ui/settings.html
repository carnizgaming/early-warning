Set up Early Warning

Enter your UK postcode or US ZIP code and the dashboard picks the right weather warnings, flood and tide gauges, power data and local news for where you live. Nothing is sent anywhere except the public feeds themselves.

UK: a full postcode is most accurate; just the first half (the district) works too and reveals less. US: a 5-digit ZIP code.
